How to play

Give teams thinking time. Say “¡Arriba!” so all teams show their boards together. Reveal the answer, then award points to every correct team. Use − to correct a score. Award up to 6 points in the memory round. Accept listed vocabulary variants; judge accents according to your lesson goals.
